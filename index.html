How It Works

From site visit to handover.

The same procedure we run internally — visible to you at every stage once you apply.

01

Site Visit. We assess your plot, access roads, and terrain before anything is priced.

02

Design. We draft a plan for you, or work from one you already have.

03

Bill of Quantities. A full costed breakdown of materials and labour, no surprises later.

04

Contract Agreement. Terms, timeline, and payment plan signed by both sides.

05

Mobilization Payment. 25%–75% deposit to begin, depending on scope.

06

Work Schedule. We prepare and share a build timeline before breaking ground.

07

Weekly Reports. You receive progress photos and updates every week, start to finish.
